About Zaras Properties

A Residential Property Investment Business Built on Quality & Integrity

Zaras Properties is a residential property investment business based in South East England, with homes across the South East and South West.

✓

Who We Are: Private residential property investors committed to raising housing standards through thoughtful refurbishment. We buy residential properties, renovate and improve them, then let and manage the homes we own.

✓

Regional Focus: Growing portfolio spanning Berkshire, Hampshire, Surrey, Oxfordshire, Bristol, Bath, and surrounding communities.

✓

Quality & Affordable Homes: Safe, affordable, well-presented, modernised homes priced fairly for long-term stability and better everyday living.

Important Clarification:

Zaras Properties is not a letting agency and does not provide letting or management services for third-party landlords. We do not sell homes—we exclusively acquire, renovate, and let properties that we own ourselves.
