OUR COMMUNITY COMMITMENT

Making room
for positive change.

Zaras Properties has a commitment to corporate social responsibility, particularly supporting rough sleepers and working with homelessness charities.

Access to safe, suitable housing matters. We recognise that homelessness is a complex challenge and that meaningful progress depends on the work of experienced charities, local organisations and communities.

Our intention is to support this work and develop constructive relationships with organisations making a practical difference.
