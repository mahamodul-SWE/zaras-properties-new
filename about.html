WHO WE ARE

Investing in homes.
Improving living.

Zaras Properties is a residential property investment business based in South East England, with properties across South East and South West England.

Our business model is straightforward: we acquire residential properties for our own portfolio, renovate and improve them, and let and manage only the properties we own. We focus on quality, affordable, safe and energy-efficient rental homes.

We are not a letting agency and do not provide letting or property management services for third-party landlords.

WHAT GUIDES US
